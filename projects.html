01 / GROWTH ANALYTICS

Who’s ready for premium?

Predicting subscription conversion from 58,000+ user records to help teams prioritize high-value users and personalize onboarding.

RClassificationCausal inference

✦ From engagement signals to growth decisions.

FIELD NOTES / 01✧
58k+user records analyzed

CONCEPT ILLUSTRATION · NOT MODEL RESULTS

Read the case study +

Problem Solved

Provided data-backed predictions of conversion probability to help teams target high-value users, personalize onboarding flows, and optimize resource allocation for subscription growth.

Technical Approach

  1. Data Engineering & Exploration — Cleaned, transformed, and validated a dataset of 58k+ user interactions. Performed EDA and causal inference checks to surface real drivers of conversion.
  2. Feature Strategy — Engineered features from engagement intensity, session timing, activity breadth, and demographics. Applied feature selection and used oversampling (SMOTE) to address class imbalance.
  3. Model Development — Built and tuned Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting (GBM/XGBoost) models using cross-validation, grid search, and threshold optimization.
  4. Evaluation & Insights — Benchmarked models, derived user-behavior clusters, and produced actionable levers for product and marketing teams.

Key Learnings

Deepened expertise in classification workflows and ensemble techniques, strengthened causal analysis practices, gained hands-on experience with balancing and hyperparameter tuning, and improved translating analytics into business recommendations.

ILLUSTRATIVE DOCUMENTS · SIMPLIFIED WORKFLOW

Read the case study +

Problem solved

Vendor risk analysts reviewed hundreds of documents manually to locate evidence and prepare assessments. The process took approximately one month and involved substantial repetitive work.

My contribution

As a Data Science Intern at Mphasis, I contributed to TracyAI by building document processing and retrieval workflows. My work connected document ingestion, relevant evidence retrieval, and evidence-grounded responses to support analysts reviewing vendor risk.

Technical approach

  1. Process documents using Python and Unstructured.io, with OCR fallback for scanned content.
  2. Generate embeddings using Amazon Titan to support retrieval of relevant document passages.
  3. Use Amazon Bedrock Nova models to generate responses grounded in retrieved evidence.
  4. Coordinate workflows using LangGraph and LangChain, with Langfuse for tracing and evaluation.

Outcome

Through the team’s work, the platform reduced assessment time from approximately one month to under seven days and cut manual effort by 60%. These are platform outcomes achieved through the team’s combined contributions.

Key takeaways

Reliable document processing and relevant retrieval are essential to useful AI responses. The experience strengthened my ability to connect AI engineering with a practical business workflow.
